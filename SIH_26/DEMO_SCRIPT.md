## ⚡ RECORDING TIPS (4x SIH winner rules)
1. **Video ≤ 3:00 strictly** — SIH auto-cuts/penalizes long videos.
2. Record screen at **1080p**, phone via emulator/scrcpy or a second camera over the shoulder (judges love a real hand tapping).
3. **Never dead-air**: if loading, pre-load screens before recording; cut with jump-cuts.
4. Add **English subtitles** (SIH requires accessible submission) + light background music at 10% volume.
5. Show **REAL taps, never a slideshow of screenshots** — one genuine live sync moment (airplane mode) is worth 10 slides.
6. First 10 seconds decide your score — practice the hook till it's flawless.
7. Keep a backup: the video file + this script + screenshots in a submission folder before deadline day.
