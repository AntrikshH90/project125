## 📸 WEBCAM TIPS (Specific to Laptop Webcam Setup)

Since your team member is recording on a **laptop with a mini webcam**, follow these exactly:

### DO THESE

- **Eye level = camera level.** Put the laptop on a stack of books if the camera is too low. Looking up at the camera = confident. Looking down = amateur.
- **Light in FRONT of your face**, not behind you. Sit facing a window or put a lamp in front. Backlighting makes you look like a silhouette.
- **Clean, plain background.** A white wall, a college poster, or a bookshelf. NOT a messy room or a bed visible.
- **Dress like you are presenting to a minister.** Solid color shirt or kurta. No patterns (they cause camera flicker). Judges notice professionalism.
- **Stay in frame — head + shoulders + chest.** Not just face. Not the full body.
- **Look at the CAMERA LENS, not the screen.** Put a small sticky arrow next to the webcam to remind yourself.
- **Speak 20% slower than normal.** You always think you're speaking slowly. You're not. Slow down more.
- **Use a wired earphone mic or clip-on mic** if available. Built-in laptop mics echo. Even a basic Rs 200 earphone dramatically improves audio quality.

### AVOID THESE

- Fan noise, AC noise, cooler sound — turn them off during recording
- Phones ringing — silent mode for ALL team members
- Bright window BEHIND you (backlight = bad)
- Reading too obviously — practice so you glance, not stare at notes
- Saying "uh", "um", "like" — replace with a silent pause
- Swaying or fidgeting — sit straight, hands on the desk (relaxed, not stiff)

## 🏅 4-TIME SIH WINNER RULES (Non-Negotiable)

1. **First 10 seconds = your score.** The hook must be flawless. Rehearse it 10 times separately.
2. **Show real taps, not screenshots.** One genuine airplane-mode sync is worth 10 slides.
3. **Never dead air.** Pre-load every tab and screen. No loading spinners on camera.
4. **English subtitles in the video.** SIH requires accessible submissions. Add via DaVinci Resolve (free) or Kapwing online.
5. **Soft background music at 8–10% volume.** Royalty-free from Pixabay. It fills silence and makes cuts feel smooth.
6. **Video ends at 2:58 or earlier.** Leave a 2-second buffer. Do not risk the cut.
7. **Export as MP4, H.264, 1080p.** Not .mov, not .avi. MP4. Portal is strict about formats.
8. **Backup EVERYTHING** the night before deadline — video, script, PPT, screenshots — on Google Drive AND a USB.
