# Create a Google Account for Gmail

A Google Account lets you sign in to Gmail and other Google services, such as YouTube, Google Play, Google Drive, and Photos.

For specific setup instructions for your Android, iPhone, or computer, [learn how to create a Google Account](/accounts/answer/27441).

## Sign up for a Google Account

![An animation showing how to create a Gmail account from accounts.google.com/signin. After the user opens the web page, they select "Create account" and browse options to create an account for their personal use, for their child, or for their business.](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/Z5aWeWGdUYQzJ4FzmIk6i4TcTwpfnMkD5Je2)

**Important:** Before you create a new Google Account, sign out of your current account. [Learn how to sign out of Gmail](/mail/answer/8154).

1.  From your device, go to the [Google Account sign in page](https://accounts.google.com/signup).
2.  Click **Create account**.
3.  Choose your account type:
    *   **For my personal use**
    *   **For my child**
    *   **For work or business**
4.  To finish, follow the on-screen instructions.

[Create a Google Account](https://accounts.google.com/signin)

**Tip:** To use Gmail for your business, a Google Workspace account might be better for you than a personal Google Account. With Google Workspace, you get increased storage, professional email addresses, and additional features. [Learn about Google Workspace pricing and plans](https://workspace.google.com/pricing.html).

[Try Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome?uj=hc-new-gmail)

## The username I want is taken

You can't register a Gmail address if the username you requested is:

*   Already being used.
*   Very similar to an existing username.
    *   For example, if example@gmail.com already exists, you can't use examp1e@gmail.com.
*   The same as a username that someone used in the past and then deleted.
*   Reserved by Google to prevent spam or abuse.

## Someone is impersonating me

If you believe someone has created a Google Account using a Gmail address to impersonate your identity, you can:

*   File a report with the [Internet Crime Complaint Center](https://www.ic3.gov).
*   Contact your state's Office of Consumer Protection.

Unfortunately, Gmail is unable to participate in mediations involving third parties regarding impersonation. [Learn more about Gmail Program Policies](/mail/answer/16734397).
