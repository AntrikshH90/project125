Our Story

BornfromFire

In the heart of Napa Valley, where the earth whispers ancient secrets of renewal, EMBER & OAK was forged from a singular obsession: the alchemy of fire, smoke, and time.

Our chef, having trained under three Michelin-starred masters across Europe, returned to his roots with one mission: to honor the ingredient, respect the flame, and create moments that linger long after the last bite.

Every cut of meat is sourced within 50 miles. Every herb is harvested from our own garden. Every flame tells a story.
